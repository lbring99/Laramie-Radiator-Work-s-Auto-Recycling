About the shop

A Laramie radiator shop and salvage yard, built for the long haul

Laramie Radiator Work's Auto Recycling has operated at 203 E Baker St since 2016, pairing a working salvage yard with a radiator and cooling-system repair shop. It's a combination that lets us pull a used part and fix a cooling system in the same visit, for everything from passenger cars to semis and farm equipment.

Customers consistently point to the same things: technicians who know what they're doing, straightforward communication, and turnaround that's often same-day — a big deal when the vehicle in question is stranded, not just inconvenienced.

  • Radiator repair, sales & install
  • Semi & heavy-truck radiators
  • Brakes & exhaust work
  • Oil coolers & AC condensers
  • Industrial, farm & mining radiators
  • Recycled parts for cars & trucks
